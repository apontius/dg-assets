The Question Beneath the Book

“I didn’t want His grace to see me through another day in the storm. I needed God to stop the storm.”

My business of fifteen years was collapsing, I faced financial ruin and a legal case that could mean prison, and the worst part was yet to come – I had to tell my wife we might lose everything.

I honestly felt like I had been checking all the right boxes in my life: praying, reading my Bible, tithing, going to church, staying faithful. I fully believed God was about to work everything out. But He didn’t. And I couldn’t stop asking, why???

Thank you for agreeing to check out the first chapter. If you are reading this right now and have ever been angry at God, disappointed with Him, or wondered whether His grace was enough, I wrote this short book for you.

Do I have all the answers? No. But I do have a few. What I can offer is sharing what helped keep me afloat when I felt like I was drowning.

I’ll send the rest of this digital copy totally free when it’s ready, just as a thank you for your feedback today.

Why I Asked You

“I will go first.”

This book bares my soul. I was brutally honest when writing it in the hopes that it will help reach and lift up those who are hurting and seemingly broken beyond repair.

Now I’m asking you to be honest with me, too. After reading chapter one, please tell me what resonated, what didn’t, and whether you are drawn to go on to chapter two.

This book was never intended to be a destination, but rather a bridge. Its purpose is to help hurting people feel less alone, and invite them to reconsider a relationship with Jesus and healthy Christian community that can walk with them in their toughest seasons of life. If Disappointing Grace accomplishes that, it has done something worthwhile and fulfilled my intentions.

— Dave
