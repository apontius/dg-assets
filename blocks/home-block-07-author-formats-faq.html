About the Author

Dave Fleet

Dave built a multimillion-dollar company, lost it all, nearly quit — but then found God in the ashes. He was put back together by Jesus, a faithful wife, and people who refused to let him stay broken.

Now he spends his life helping others find their way back. He wrote Disappointing Grace because he believes there are a lot of people hurting quietly who've drifted from God and just need someone to say, "me too." The audiobook is read in his own voice, because some things land better heard than read.

"If my story helps even one person stop hiding and start healing — it was worth telling."

A Few Questions

Before you begin

Can I get the whole book free?

Yes. Choose “Read Free” or “Listen Free” and enter your name and email to access the full eBook or audiobook through BookFunnel. Updates from Dave are optional — the book is free either way. If you opt in, you can unsubscribe at any time. The Kindle edition is a separate purchase on Amazon for readers who prefer that option and want to support the launch.

Do I need a Kindle device?

No. You can read the Kindle edition using Amazon's Kindle app on a phone or tablet, as well as on a Kindle device.

Can I try it before choosing a format?

Yes. Look inside the book or listen to the Chapter 1 audio preview near the top of this page.

Is this for someone who has drifted from church?

Yes. Dave wrote for people who are hurting quietly, wrestling with unanswered prayer, or unsure how to find their way back. His story points toward Jesus and the people who can walk alongside you.

Can we use it with a group?

The book includes a reader's guide for personal reflection or group discussion. For church, ministry, or bulk-copy questions, contact the team.
