Pay It Forward

Help carry hope to the next person.

An honest question in a men’s group opened a conversation Dave hadn’t expected. Now you can help that conversation reach someone else.

Dave Fleet, author and narrator of Disappointing Grace.

It started with an honest question.

In a men’s group Dave helped facilitate, he asked, “Can God’s grace ever be disappointing?” Men began talking about times they were angry at God, doubted Him, or felt ignored.

Dave realized other people might be carrying those same feelings without knowing how to talk about them. So he wrote honestly about his own.
