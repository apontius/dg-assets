A few questions

Before you pay it forward

Where does my contribution go?

Contributions support the work of making the audiobook available free and helping it reach more people, including delivery tools, outreach, and project operating costs. They are not assigned to a specific recipient or a guaranteed number of copies.

Do I have to contribute to read or share the book?

No. The full eBook and audiobook are available free. You can share the book’s website with someone whether or not you contribute.

Is my contribution tax-deductible?

No. This is voluntary financial support for the Disappointing Grace project, not a tax-deductible charitable donation.

Can I choose my own contribution amount?

Yes. When you continue to Stripe, you can enter the amount you would like to contribute. This is a one-time contribution.

How does someone receive the free book?

Send them the book’s website. They can choose “Read or listen free” and sign up to receive the eBook and audiobook through BookFunnel.

Can my church or small group share it?

Yes. You can share the free digital book with your group. For print copies or questions about using the book in your ministry, contact team@disappointinggrace.com.
