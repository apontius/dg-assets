Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Disappointing Grace ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit www.disappointinggrace.com and any associated pages (the "Site") or purchase our products.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Information you provide directly: When you sign up for our email list, make a purchase, or contact us, we collect your name, email address, and any other information you choose to provide.
  • Payment information: When you make a purchase, your payment is processed securely by Stripe. We do not store your full credit card number, expiration date, or CVV on our servers. Stripe's privacy policy governs how your payment data is handled.
  • Automatically collected information: We may collect basic analytics data such as pages visited, referring URLs, and device type to understand how visitors use the Site. This data is collected in aggregate and is not used to personally identify you.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Fulfill purchases and deliver products (paperback, eBook, or audiobook)
  • Send email communications you've signed up for, including book updates, launch announcements, and related content
  • Respond to questions or messages you send us
  • Improve the Site and understand how it is used

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Email Communications

If you provide your email address, you may receive messages related to the book, including launch updates, content from Dave, and related announcements. Every email includes an unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time, and we will promptly honor your request.

Our email is delivered through MailerLite. Their privacy policy is available at mailerlite.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Payment Processing

All payments are processed securely by Stripe. When you make a purchase, your payment information is transmitted directly to Stripe using industry-standard encryption (TLS/SSL). We do not have access to your full card details.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate the Site:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • MailerLite — email delivery and site hosting

Each service has its own privacy policy governing how they handle your data.

Cookies

The Site may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and analytics. You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings. The Site will function normally with cookies disabled, though some features may be limited.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The Site is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly remove it.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at the email address below. We will respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.

Changes to This Policy
